LinkedIn Video Campaign Report

Client: KIMMCO-ISOVER Reporting Period: October 8, 2026 – November 6, 2026

1. Executive Summary

Between October 8 and November 6, 2026, a top-of-funnel LinkedIn campaign was executed with the objective of maximizing Video Views. The campaign utilized a daily budget of $23.00 to distribute non-combustible insulation messaging to a broad professional audience, focusing on generating high impression volume and video plays at a fractional cost per view.

3. Campaign Targeting & Delivered Audience

Locations Targeted: Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain.

The campaign reached a broad professional audience across these regions, with impressions distributed across the following primary job functions:

  • Operations: 10.69% of impressions (45,965 impressions / 43,280 views)
  • Sales: 5.17% of impressions (22,210 impressions / 20,960 views)
  • Engineering: 5.06% of impressions (21,754 impressions / 20,153 views)
  • Education: 3.11% of impressions (13,354 impressions / 12,838 views)
  • Healthcare Services: 2.77% of impressions (11,099 impressions / 11,329 views)

Completion Insight: The Operations demographic recorded the highest raw number of full video completions (683) with a completion rate of 1.49%.

4. Creative & Messaging Overview

Promoted Video: "Real Non-Combustible Insulation"

  • Headline: Real Non-Combustible Insulation
  • Primary Copy: "A moment's discomfort for a lifetime of safety. KIMMCO-ISOVER's non-combustible glass wool insulation protects the people inside your buildings when it matters most. The itch fades, but the fire safety lasts for the life of the building. Itchy is good. Safe is better. Choose wisely for your next project."
  • Call to Action (CTA): Learn more
  • Analysis: The creative drove a high volume of views at a fractional cent per play ($0.0005), fulfilling the campaign's core brand visibility objective.

5. Recommendations & Next Steps

  • Retargeting Opportunity: Given the large pool of video viewers (410,235), consider creating a retargeting audience segment in LinkedIn Campaign Manager to serve lead generation or direct inquiry ads specifically to professionals who engaged with or completed this video.
  • Focus on High-Performing Segments: Operations and Engineering generated the highest engagement and completions. Future video allocations should prioritize these functions.
